# Scholarly Prose Editing

Produces an authoritative, readable editorial revision that human authors can verify and defend.

## Principles of Scholarly Editing

1. **Preserve the Evidence Base**: Never modify or smooth empirical numbers, p-values, sample sizes ($N$), confidence intervals, drug dosages, or outcome definitions.
2. **Eliminate Wordiness & Jargon**: Replace convoluted passive nominalizations with direct, active verbs where scientifically accurate.
3. **Preserve Hedging & Epistemic Humility**: Retain authors' exact degree of certainty (e.g. "suggests" vs "proves", "in this murine model" vs "in humans").
4. **Citation Stability**: Ensure all citation markers (`[@AuthorYear]`) remain firmly attached to their corresponding claims during revisions.
