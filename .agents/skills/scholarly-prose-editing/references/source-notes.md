# Sources and interpretation

Checked 7 October 2026. These sources inform editing and report interpretation; they do not prescribe a detector-evasion method.

- [Wikipedia Signs of AI writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing): community advice, not a peer-reviewed diagnostic standard or Wikipedia policy. It discusses generic claims, exaggerated significance, formulaic prose, vague attribution, and citation/markup defects. Such patterns can occur in human writing. Use them to improve clarity and source integrity, not to infer authorship from punctuation or vocabulary.
- [Crossref Similarity Check](https://www.crossref.org/services/similarity-check/): a publishing workflow powered by iThenticate that identifies overlap with other material for editorial assessment. This supports choosing a journal-oriented similarity service; it does not establish a ranking of all detectors or a universal passing score.
- [iThenticate Similarity Report guide](https://guides.ithenticate.com/hc/en-us/articles/27838877807245-Overview-of-the-new-Similarity-Report-experience): matches can arise from quotations and bibliographies. Assess the matched passages, attribution, and report settings; the percentage alone does not establish plagiarism.
- [Turnitin AI Writing Report guide](https://guides.turnitin.com/hc/en-us/articles/22774058814093-Using-the-AI-Writing-Report): the AI indicator concerns qualifying prose and is distinct from similarity. Nonzero scores below 20 percent are not numerically displayed because of increased false-positive concerns. This threshold is not a general journal acceptance rule.
- [iThenticate AI report guide](https://guides.ithenticate.com/hc/en-us/articles/27835303398541-AI-writing-detection-in-the-Similarity-Report): consult the actual organizational account to establish whether AI detection is enabled. Do not assume every similarity report includes it.
- [iThenticate product and individual credits](https://www.ithenticate.com/): the individual packages listed at checking did not include AI detection; organizational offerings differ. Recheck access and terms rather than recommending a purchase for an unavailable feature.
- [ICMJE authors and contributors](https://www.icmje.org/recommendations/browse/roles-and-responsibilities/defining-the-role-of-authors-and-contributors.html) and [ICMJE use of AI by authors](https://www.icmje.org/recommendations/browse/artificial-intelligence/ai-use-by-authors.html): human authors remain responsible for accuracy, attribution, and appropriate disclosure; AI tools cannot assume authorship responsibility. Verify the selected journal's current requirements before submission.

Editorial decisions such as preserving exact denominators, recording incomplete review procedures, and comparing citation anchors are manuscript-integrity practices. They are not validated AI-detection indicators.
