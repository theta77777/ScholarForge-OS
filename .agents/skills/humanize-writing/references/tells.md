# Tell inventory (reference for the audit step)

No single item here proves AI authorship. Several clustered in one paragraph is the signal.
Fix the cause (Layer 1/2 in SKILL.md), not the surface word.

## Content-level (strongest)
- Generic claims that survive a noun swap into another topic
- Significance inflation: small fact -> "reflects a broader trend", "underscores the importance of"
- Vague attribution: "experts say", "studies show", "some argue" with no named source
- Both-sides hedging on every point; no position anywhere
- Closing sentence that restates the paragraph's point ("Overall, ...", "In essence, ...")
- Promotional or travel-brochure tone in neutral contexts ("rich", "vibrant", "stunning")

## Structure-level
- Every paragraph the same length (3-5 sentences) and same shape: topic sentence, support, wrap-up
- Sentences within a paragraph all 18-26 words
- Same sentence frame reused across a document (e.g. "X cannot establish Y" ten times)
- Symmetrical negation: "not just X, but Y"; "less about X, more about Y"
- Rule of three everywhere; triplet adjectives, triplet lists
- Bold-lead bullets whose bold text repeats the sentence
- Title Case Headings, emoji headings, "Key Takeaways" boxes
- Paragraph openers that are all connectors: Moreover / Furthermore / Additionally / Notably / Importantly

## Word-level (weakest alone)
delve, tapestry, landscape (figurative), realm, navigate (figurative), foster, leverage, harness, robust,
comprehensive, pivotal, crucial, vital, seamless, multifaceted, nuanced (as filler), intricate, testament,
underscore, highlight (verb, for "shows"), showcase, boast, garner, align with, in the realm of,
it is worth noting, it is important to note, plays a (key|vital|pivotal|crucial) role, a wide range of,
serves as, stands as, ever-evolving, game-changer, shed light on, paves the way, sheds light

## Academic-prose specific
- "This review examines / explores / sheds light on" openers mirrored in every section
- Repeated limitation boilerplate ("cannot establish", "remains unresolved", "requires further...") in nearly every paragraph
- Passive voice + nominalization stacks ("the assessment of the determination of")
- Uniform, hedge-everything register with zero concrete asides

## Not tells (leave alone)
- Em dashes as ordinary punctuation
- Lists where the content really is a list
- Correct grammar, consistent spelling, domain jargon the field actually uses
- Semicolons in academic writing
