# Research methods by field and question

Read the relevant sections; verify current primary reporting/appraisal guidance before selecting instruments. This is a decision aid, not a frozen standards manual.

## Design and contribution

A critical narrative review interprets explicitly chosen evidence without claiming exhaustive retrieval. A systematic review needs a focused question, reproducible identification/selection, extraction/appraisal and justified synthesis. A scoping review maps concepts/evidence types with transparent eligibility. An umbrella review uses existing reviews and needs overlap assessment. Meta-analysis is an analysis method, not decorative proof of rigour.

Before formal selection record question, eligibility, sources, extraction fields, analytical units, appraisal and synthesis rules. Preserve dated amendments and consequences. Define AI/human roles. Use PICO/PECO/PCC where suitable, not automatically for interpretive humanities. Check registration eligibility; do not invent a registration ID or call retrospective work prospective.

Competitor matrix: citation/identifier; actual question/eligibility; sources/search-end date; findings/limits; proposed contribution; supporting locator; access state. A new title or recent date is not novelty. Inaccessible competitors stay unresolved.

## Field routing

| Field | Discovery routes, subject to actual access | Evidence distinctions |
|---|---|---|
| Clinical/biomedical | PubMed/MEDLINE interface, discipline indexes, Europe PMC retrieval, trial registries/regulators, citation discovery | Preclinical/human; donor/recipient; treatment/association; protocol/results; biological n; related cohorts |
| Engineering/materials/chemistry | Discipline libraries/publisher indexes, standards bodies, relevant patents, Crossref/OpenAlex, citations | Composition/settings, comparator/units, lifecycle boundary, batches/replicates, standards version, laboratory/field |
| Computing/AI | Conference/journal libraries, arXiv with later-version checks, datasets/benchmark documentation, repositories | Dataset overlap/leakage, task/metric, model version, compute/baseline fairness, seeds, benchmark/deployment |
| Social science/education/economics | Discipline indexes, authoritative working papers and survey/institutional sources, citations | Sampling/context, construct/instrument, confounding/identification, qualitative/quantitative aims, time design |
| Humanities/law/history | Books/library catalogues, scholarly journals, primary archives and authoritative legal sources/editions | Edition/translation, jurisdiction/date, source/interpretation, corpus/method; no forced clinical quality score |
| Environment/interdisciplinary | Relevant indexes, official datasets/standards, institutional sources, citations | Spatial/temporal scale, scenario/system boundary, observed/modelled results, baselines/dependent datasets |

Web/repositories complement discovery; their coverage is not an executed subscription search. Preserve interface, exact executable query, date/date field, filters, counts/export completeness, raw file and problems. Seed retrieval is diagnostic, not proof of completeness.

## Records and extraction

Use stable report R, study/dataset S, outcome E and claim C IDs. Keep them when citation numbers change. Separate discovered, screened, sought, not retrieved, read, extracted, appraised, excluded-with-reason and awaiting-decision states. A bibliography is not an included-study count.

Verify bibliographic identity with original/authoritative records: author/title/year, publication/volume/pages or article ID, DOI where present, or ISBN/edition/stable URL. Record source/date, preprint-to-final relationship, correction type/effect. A DOI verifies neither an extracted result nor complete publisher-notice clearance. Preserve superseded values.

Important findings need exact locators and design/context; population/object/version; exposure/settings; comparator; endpoint; sample size and analytical unit; timeframe; estimate/units/uncertainty; adjustment; attrition; funding/conflicts where material; overlap; access and appraisal state. Distinguish donors from recipient animals, islets from donors, technical replicates from independent batches, events from people, and model seeds from datasets. Missing stays unknown, not zero. Preserve disagreements across abstract/text/tables/supplements. Calculations require original values, formula and denominator.

Do not sum overlapping cohorts or compare benchmarks/operating conditions as if identical. Link study families before pooling. Maintain search/screening/source/evidence/appraisal/claim records separately; extend fields for the discipline without inventing entries to fill templates.

## Appraisal and synthesis

Use current design/outcome-matched tools with domain judgments, reasons/locators and reviewer type. Reporting completeness is not risk of bias. Applicability, precision and certainty differ. Do not invent scalar study-quality scores or force an intervention-effect certainty framework onto descriptive/interpretive questions. AI judgments remain identified until required human review.

Before pooling compare populations/objects, interventions/settings, outcome definitions, follow-up, metrics, adjustment and dependence. Use reproducible analysis and justified variance/conversion handling; do not invent missing variances. Narrative synthesis links conclusions to sources and explains disagreement. Nonsignificance is not equivalence, significance counts are not votes, and inferred causality needs appropriate evidence. Proposals stay proposals until validated.
