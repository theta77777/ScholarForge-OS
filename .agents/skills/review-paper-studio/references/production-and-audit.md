# Production and completion gates

Main PDF/DOCX contain the article, legitimate known author facts, abstract/keywords, appropriate sections, tables/figures/captions, truthful required declarations and references. Put declarations before References; end at the last reference. Keep action lists, operational instructions, prompts and audit scores separate. Scientific limitations and assistance disclosures remain in the paper.

Supporting PDF/DOCX distinguish Part A scientific supplement from Part B author/audit/provenance working records. Part B is not automatically a journal-upload supplement. Research/artwork ZIP retains raw queries/exports, protocol/amendments, registers, evidence/claims/appraisal, verified reference library, figures/code and audit. Exclude temporary QA files and conflicting old final manuscripts unless intentionally labelled historical snapshots.

## Production

Use the host's document/PDF tools and contracts. build_document.py supports basic Markdown headings, paragraphs, tables, images and numbered references with actual Times New Roman font files; it is not a universal typesetter. Complex equations/structures/footnotes/templates require a suitable richer engine. Never leave raw LaTeX/Markdown in final pages.

Prefer one verified editable source and reliable PDF conversion when available. Native Word/LibreOffice can export DOCX. Direct PDF generation is acceptable when final content/order match even if wrapping/page counts differ. Discover actual fonts; Times-Roman is not Times New Roman. Name substitutions instead of asserting exact formatting.

Use deliberate table widths, expanding rows and repeated headers. Pair captions/artwork, prevent orphan headings, verify Greek/micro/math glyphs, page numbers and references. End-of-text figures/tables may be journal manuscript organization, not padding. Never reduce requested text size to meet count limits.

audit_pdf.py creates mechanical properties and optional page PNGs plus pending VISUAL_REVIEW.<hash>.json. Open each page before recording inspection. Old-PDF review cannot certify the new hash. Record AI inspection honestly; it is not human scientific approval. Diagnose missing render/view tools and use a working alternative or state the limitation. Re-render after corrections and inspect latest affected pages; text extraction alone is insufficient.

## Audits

1. Source/numerical: identity/support, locators, units/denominators, arithmetic/uncertainty, corrections/overlap.
2. Methods/interpretation: actual/planned procedures, reconciled selection counts, access/appraisal state, causal/comparative claims, proposals, negative results.
3. Journal/editorial: scope/type/eligibility, current limits/count basis, assistance/artwork policy, fees, prose, declarations/author facts.
4. Integrated: abstract/body/table/figure/supplement alignment, numbering, protected evidence qualifications, word budgets, output equality, reference boundary, latest layout/files/downloads.

Log severity/location, source evidence, root cause, repair, dependent content rechecked and uncertainty. Critical defects threaten validity/integrity; major defects weaken reproducibility/interpretation/fit; minor defects affect presentation. A changed denominator requires related percentages/tables/abstract/conclusion checks. More loops do not prove quality.

Give evidence for deductions/increases; identify unassessable parts and missing human work. No rescaling, invented pass, acceptance probability or claim that this is a validated scientific rating. Aim for 90+ only where actual work permits. A high score cannot override a critical defect. An honest complete working draft with named requirements is a legitimate final output.

## Final gates

Evidence supports the actual question; no fabricated references/results, concealed methods or misleading novelty. Important claims/tables/figures trace to sources. Performed/incomplete procedures and AI/human roles are explicit. Budgets/journal rules reconcile and diagram labels are counted. Main paper ends at references; supporting records are separate; declarations are factual. All latest pages/figures actually inspected, files reopen, content/order agree and no known layout defect remains. Human responsibility, author facts and source/figure validation determine readiness.

Final response gives actual files, counts, completed checks, management score with limits, status and necessary author actions. Save STATUS.md at checkpoints and resume it. Do not claim background execution, submission, contacts, deposits or spending unless separately authorized.
