# Evidence-led writing and scientific artwork

Outline an argument from extraction. Introduction connects the actual problem, prior syntheses, remaining contribution and objective. Methods describe performed work. Evidence/discussion explain mechanisms, measurement, disagreement, comparability, uncertainty and implications. Conclusion stays proportionate. Adapt headings/abstract to the design and verified journal, not a fixed biomedical template.

For important claims and numeric cells/captions, record text/location, stable report/outcome IDs, citations, direct/contextual/inferential support, exact locators, limitations and verification. Related-subject citations are insufficient. Abstract/conclusion synthesis can link several sources with clause checks where necessary. Cite credible opposing results; preserve scope and negative findings.

Write natural scholarly prose without inflated novelty, repetitive staging or generic future-work lists. Introduce technical terms when useful. Preserve citations, numbers, units, timepoints, exceptions and definitions during polishing. Do not add invented human experience, optimize detector percentages or conceal assistance. Audit factual drift after language edits.

Tables should compare common attributes or summarize interpretations with source support. Include comparator/conditions/analytical unit and key limits. Missing stays unknown. Original synthesis is not a reproduced publisher table; copied/adapted content requires appropriate attribution/permissions. Put detailed source extraction in support rather than tiny unreadable cells.

## Figure contract

Before drawing specify question/inference, conceptual/empirical status, panels/labels/arrow meanings, sources/data, caption/qualifications, print width and current permitted artwork policy. Number follows need. A mechanism/barrier sequence, meaningful comparison or proposed evaluation framework often helps; do not recycle topic-specific biology across unrelated fields.

Use editable vector drawing/code with assets/figure_style.json as a starting style. Prefer white background, restrained colours, aligned panels, consistent 10–12 pt labels at print size and enough whitespace. Use line styles/wording for uncertainty, not colour alone. Avoid decorative gradients, shadows, pseudo-microscopy, 3D ornament and unexplained molecular icons.

Arrows distinguish observed mechanisms, hypotheses, losses, inhibition and proposed routes. Schematics do not imply measured fractions, exclusive specificity or causality without data. Comparison figures do not establish superiority from incomparable studies. Proposed frameworks are labelled proposed. Verify biology/chemistry/physics/geography/computational topology according to the field.

Empirical plots require traceable data, units/denominators/time, uncertainty and reproducible analysis. Literature trend plots need an actual query/dataset. PRISMA flows require reconciled completed counts and correct units. Do not fabricate unavailable measurements, curves, microscopy, chemical structures or screening numbers.

Keep SVG/equivalent masters and drawing/analysis code where used. Export accepted vector formats and suitable PNG/TIFF; normally at least 600 effective dpi for line diagrams. Calculate pixels/intended inches: DPI metadata alone is insufficient. Use compatible Word fallback while retaining masters. Check actual PDF sharpness and minimum label size.

Captions explain panels, symbols/line styles, nonstandard abbreviations, status, sources, inference limits and required assistance/permission disclosures. Synchronize callouts/numbering. Maintain per-figure provenance and source/data map. Reproducible AI-assisted drawing remains AI-assisted even without an image generator. Human artist work or validation cannot be invented. Verify current publisher policy before drawing, retain verifiable inputs and disclose accurately.

Inspect standalone figures at high resolution and final page images at intended print size: labels, line collisions, arrowheads, panel order, symbols, colour/contrast, alt text, captions and citations. Record actual AI/human verification states separately.
